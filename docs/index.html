Methodology

  • Haircut = 1 − mostRecentValuationAmount / valuationSecuritizationAmount. Pool and group haircuts are value-weighted.
  • Maturity wall = balance with maturityDate in 2026–2028 (inclusive), over total current balance (currentEndingScheduledBalanceAmount).
  • Distress: DSCR (mostRecentDebtServiceCoverageNetOperatingIncomePercentage) < 1.00x; occupancy (mostRecentPhysicalOccupancyPercentage) < 75%; special servicing = a special-servicer transfer date is reported.
  • Weighted DSCR / occupancy are balance-weighted over loans that report the metric and carry a positive balance.
  • Metro is assigned from propertyCity + propertyState via a fixed lookup; unmatched cities fall into “Other”.
